ON CALL privacy policy

Contact: nomenstudiogames@gmail.com

ON CALL is a single-player tower defense game for Android. This page explains what data the game uses and why. We don't run our own servers, and we don't collect your name, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your progress

Your progress is saved on your device and may be included in your Android device backup.

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your game progress is also stored in your Google Play Games Saved Games, so it can follow you to another device. Google provides your Play Games player ID and display name to the game for this. Signing in is optional; you can play as a guest, in which case progress stays only on your device. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob. When you choose to watch one, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), app interactions and diagnostics to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Gameplay statistics

To balance stages and fix difficulty spikes, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to PostHog, an analytics service, on servers in the EU. These include which stages you play and how runs end, your level, coins, shop purchases (in-game coins only), talent choices and rewarded videos watched. Each install gets a random ID that isn't linked to your name, Google account, Play Games ID or advertising ID. PostHog sees your IP address when the game connects and uses it to estimate your country; IP addresses aren't stored. You can turn this off any time in Settings → Share usage stats, which also deletes any statistics not yet sent from your device.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.

Deleting your data

ON CALL by Nomen Studio keeps very little data. To delete it:

  • Progress on your device: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → ON CALL → Storage → Clear data. This deletes all progress, settings and coins saved on the device immediately.
  • Cloud progress (only if you signed in with Google Play Games): open the Google Play Games app → Profile → Settings → "Delete Play Games account & data", and delete the data for ON CALL. Or email us at nomenstudiogames@gmail.com from your Google account's address with the subject "Delete my ON CALL data", and we'll walk you through it within 30 days.
  • Gameplay statistics: turn off Settings → Share usage stats to stop sending them. They're tied only to a random install ID, so we can't match them to you, and they're kept for up to 12 months.
  • Ad data: we don't store any. Google AdMob's data is handled under Google's policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

We don't keep any copy of your data after it's deleted, because we don't run our own servers.
